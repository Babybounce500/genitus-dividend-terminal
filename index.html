How This Works

Confirmed payers only

This terminal only lists symbols that have at least one real, confirmed dividend payment on record. Every row here was individually checked against the live dividend history endpoint — nothing is guessed or estimated.

Yield %

Sum of all payments in the trailing 12 months, divided by the current price. Sortable — click the column header, or use the min-yield filter to cut the noise.

Streak

Consecutive calendar years where the total annual payout did not decrease vs. the year before. A quick, honest read on payout stability — not a prediction.

Scan scope & caching

A full exchange can hold thousands of tickers, so the scan runs live and progressively — results appear as they're confirmed, and you can stop anytime and keep what's found. Every scanned exchange is cached per-browser (24h), so re-opening it later is instant instead of re-scanning from zero.
